Common questions

Do you offer delivery?

Local delivery is available within a 1–2 state radius of Virginia, depending on schedule and weather.

How do deposits work?

Deposits reserve a chinchilla for 7 days. Balance is due at pickup. We accept cash, Zelle, and WeChat Pay when available.

Can I meet the chinchilla first?

Yes. We can schedule a short meet-and-greet, usually evenings or weekends.

Reserve or ask a question

Send a request and we will respond within 24 hours. Include the chinchilla name from the listing.
